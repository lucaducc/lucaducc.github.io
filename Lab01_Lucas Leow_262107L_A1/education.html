Riverside Primary School

CCA: Football

For my primary education, I attended Riverside Primary School, where I learnt Math, English, Mother Tongue Language (Chinese) and Science, getting many awards and titles, both for academics and for leadership such as Top In Cohort, Prefect etc.

I joined the football CCA in Primary 3, participating in the National School Games in Primary 4 and 5, before COVID-19 hit, where it was cancelled in Primary 6. In these school games, I managed to achieve 3rd in North Zone, qualifying for the Nationals, but ultimately losing in the group stage. Overall, in my CCA, I learned how to work as a team, and improved on my leadership.

Chung Cheng High School (Main)

CCA: National Police Cadet Corps

For my secondary education, I attended Chung Cheng High School (Main), where I learnt many different subjects, including the ones already learnt in primary school, along with some additional ones. In school, I was class leader in Secondary 4, helping my class plan events and activities like Teacher's Day, Graduation, and Class Outings. I also did many volunteer projects, such as helping an elderly home, packing food and much more. My experience in CCHMS helped me to become more compassionate, a better leader and disciplined to do my work.

I joined NPCC in Secondary 1, being my 2nd choice, I was excited to join it from the start. In this CCA, I helped my school win Distinction as a squad every year that I was in it, increasing a long streak of awards my school had achieved. Although the highest rank I achieved was Sergeant, I was still selected for multiple competitions, open houses and much more. Through this CCA, I learned discipline, leadership and perseverance. This CCA was an extremely vital stepping point to help me become the student I am today.

Nanyang Polytechnic

Why I joined NYP

For my tertiary education, I am currently studying in Nanyang Polytechnic, studying for a Diploma in Game Development and Technology.

Coding had really interested me since young, and once I heard this course was the most in-depth in all of Singapore, I had to check it out. During open house, I was amazed by the NYP atmosphere, and immediately knew that this was the school for me.
